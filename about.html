About me

About me

I like figuring out how things work and putting that knowledge into practice.

How I like to work

Computer vision interests me because of what it can help us understand about the physical world. I enjoy testing ideas, debugging code and working with hardware.

I like having responsibility for a task and enough time to work through it. I also value feedback and talking through decisions with the people I work with.

I care about useful technology, including work that supports people, the environment and animals.

Living in Norway and Germany

I enjoyed the nature and lifestyle in Norway. In Germany, I liked the large technology scene and the forests around Kaiserslautern.

My background

I studied Electrical Engineering with a focus on Cybernetics at the University of Tehran, then completed the Erasmus Mundus joint master’s in Embedded Computing Systems at NTNU and RPTU, following the AI track.

I’ve worked on biometrics, computer architecture, mixed-reality robot control and applied AI. My thesis is the project I feel most connected to. I worked on the camera processing, server and database, and learned a lot from getting them to work together.

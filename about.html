The person behind the work

Curious. Hands-on.
Better together.

I enjoy understanding how things work, and turning that understanding into something people can use.

What keeps me interested

Computer vision appeals to me because it connects the digital world to the physical one. I enjoy the practical side of that connection: debugging, working with hardware, testing an idea and seeing what actually happens.

I like taking responsibility for a piece of work, making progress, then exchanging feedback and thinking through the next decisions together. The shared goal matters to me.

I care about useful technology, including work that supports people, the environment and animals.

Two places that shaped me

In Norway, I enjoyed the nature and the lifestyle. In Germany, I loved how much space technology has in everyday life and work, as well as the forests around Kaiserslautern. Both experiences are part of what I bring with me.

My route into this work

I studied Electrical Engineering with a focus on Cybernetics at the University of Tehran, then completed the Erasmus Mundus joint master’s in Embedded Computing Systems at NTNU and RPTU, following the AI track.

My experience has taken me through biometric research, computer architecture, mixed-reality robot control and applied AI. My master’s thesis is the project I most identify with: it asked me to think about the entire system, from camera-side processing to the server and database.
